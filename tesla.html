6-DOF Robot Arm Design

Technical project under Professor Daniella Constantescu focused on the complete engineering design and virtual prototyping of a low-cost, manufacturing-scalable 6-DOF tabletop robotic arm:

  • Deriving the manipulator kinematics and workspace, including forward and inverse kinematics, joint-to-end-effector velocity relationships, and full joint range-of-motion simulation.
  • Developing the mechanical design package, including link sizing, joint and bearing design, fastener standardization, bill of materials, and actuator selection based on calculated loads and safety margins.
  • Creating the electrical wiring and power architecture, fully mated motion-capable CAD assembly, and virtual Jog/Teach interface to demonstrate interactive robot control without requiring a physical prototype.
